About Our Cabins

Luxury Rooms

At Silver Springs Inn & Resort, our hand-crafted log cabins offer a luxurious rustic escape complete with cozy stone fireplaces, private decks, and spectacular mountain views. For those who prefer a more authentic wilderness experience, our secluded campgrounds feature spacious, well-equipped tent and RV sites nestled right along the tranquil river banks. Whichever stay you choose, you will be fully immersed in the pristine beauty and peaceful serenity of the Rocky Mountains.

Amenities

Our resort elevates your mountain getaway with premium amenities designed for ultimate relaxation and adventure. Guests can unwind after a day on the trails in our bubbling outdoor hot tubs, gather around the communal stone fire pits for evening s’mores, or take advantage of our complimentary mountain bike rentals to explore the local ridges. We also offer fully stocked grilling stations and high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the property, ensuring you stay connected while enjoying the great outdoors.

Endless Activities

Beyond the comfort of your cabin, a world of outdoor adventure awaits with scenic hiking and horseback riding trails accessible directly from the property. Guests can spend their afternoons fly fishing in our pristine mountain streams or join our guided morning wildlife tours to spot elk and eagles in their natural habitat. As night falls, our open-sky clearings offer some of the most breathtaking, unobstructed stargazing views in the entire Rocky Mountain region.
