Policies

Check-In, Check-Out, and Quiet Hours

To preserve the peaceful serenity of our alpine sanctuary, check-in begins at 4:00 PM to allow our team to meticulously prepare your cabin, while check-out is required by 11:00 AM on your departure date. We ask that all guests respect our designated quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Sound travels easily across the mountain ridges, and this policy ensures everyone can enjoy the crisp night air and the soothing rustle of the pines without disruption. Unapproved late check-outs will incur a fee, as we must remain on schedule to welcome incoming travelers seeking their wilderness escape.

Wildlife Safety and Food Storage

Nestled deep in the heart of the Rocky Mountains, our resort is a shared habitat with majestic local wildlife, including elk, moose, and black bears. For your safety and the preservation of these animals, all guests must strictly adhere to our wildlife protocol. No food, trash, or scented items may be left outdoors on cabin decks or in unsecured vehicles at any time. Every cabin is equipped with bear-proof trash receptacles, and we require all garbage to be disposed of immediately in these designated bins. Feeding or approaching wildlife is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate eviction, as keeping a safe distance protects both you and the ecosystem.

Fire Safety and Outdoor Grilling

Given our pristine, heavily forested location, fire safety is our absolute highest priority. Open campfires, wood-burning fire pits, and fireworks are strictly banned across the entire property. Guests are welcome to use the designated propane fire tables and community grilling stations provided by the resort, provided they are never left unattended while in use. In the event of high wind speeds or county-enforced fire bans, all outdoor cooking units will be locked down immediately. We appreciate your vigilance in helping us protect these beautiful mountains and our historic log cabins from the devastating threat of wildfires.
